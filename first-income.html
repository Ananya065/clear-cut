UX Concept Design · YouTube Creator Ecosystem

First Income

Helping first-time creators in India see where they stand on the way to YouTube monetization, and what to do next.

Scope note: This is a concept design based on secondary research. I did not run interviews or user tests for this project. Every finding below is labeled with its source type, and my next step is testing with real creators.

Problem

First-time creators in India face several monetization paths at once: an early tier, full ad revenue sharing, Shorts-based routes, memberships and Super Thanks. The rules differ by route and change over time, so a new creator can't easily tell which thresholds apply to them or what to do next.

Evidence (secondary research)

1. The rules are described inconsistently.
Public guides still quote the classic 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours rule, while others describe an expanded early tier (500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, 3,000 watch hours or 3 million Shorts views) and a separate Shorts route. Source type: creator-facing blogs and news (upgrowth.in, payu.in, lilachbullock.com, Business Standard). Verify current numbers on YouTube Help.
2. Several routes, one dashboard question: “where am I?”
Eligibility has three or more variables (subscribers, watch hours or Shorts views, uploads, policy status), and ad revenue sharing needs a higher tier than fan funding. Source type: same guides.
3. Rules change, so guides go stale.
The program was widened in 2023 and further changes have been reported for 2027. Source type: news (sharesansar.com, indiatvnews.com). Treat as reported, not verified.

Limits: I reviewed a small set of public pages, not a systematic sample of creator discussions.

Design hypotheses (to test, not proven)

  • H1: Creators quit partly because the rules and their own progress are unclear.
  • H2: A single “next step” tracker will help more than additional explanatory text.

Concept: the First Income tracker

Your route

Pick: Videos, Shorts, or Both. The tracker then shows only the thresholds that apply.

Progress
Subscribers 320 / 500
Watch hours 1,900 / 3,000
Sample numbers for illustration only.
One next step

“Upload 1 more public video in the next 30 days.” Earning tools unlock in plain language as each tier is reached.

Design principles

PrincipleHow it shows up
Show statusProgress against the creator’s own route, not every rule at once
Plain languageShort copy suited to first-time, non-native English readers, with Hindi as a later option
One actionA single recommended next step on the screen
AccessibilityText contrast and tap-target sizes to be checked against WCAG 2.2 AA

What I would do next

  • Interview 5 to 8 small creators about how they learned the rules.
  • Run a 5-person usability test of the tracker.
  • Check the concept against YouTube’s design system and Help documentation.
